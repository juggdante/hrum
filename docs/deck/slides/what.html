What HRUM does

Four questions a CHRO keeps asking

Cost

What do we pay, to whom, and how is it trending?

Fairness

Is pay consistent within the same job?

Risk

Who is likely to leave, why, and what does it cost?

Ask

Plain-language questions, answers that show their sources.

REAL data

SF Employee Compensation (public, PDDL 1.0): 1,180,053 pay records, fiscal years 2013 to 2026.

SYNTHETIC data

An HRIS layer for 40,344 employees (hire and leave dates, level, rating). Every result from it is labelled.

The data honesty rule matters: compensation is real public data from the City and County of San Francisco, but turnover data does not exist publicly, so the HRIS layer is generated. The app, the agent and the charts label anything synthetic, and the attrition model is presented as a demonstration of method, not a finding about San Francisco.
