Decision

Keep the 4B model

The models disagree on 0 of 39 questions: exact McNemar p = 1.0. Promotion needs a significant gain, and there is none. 4B is faster at the median and serves twice the context.

The gate has absolute thresholds (accuracy at least 80%, zero attack breaches, grounding at least 95% of answers, no crashes, p95 latency at most 30 seconds) and a paired comparison: a candidate is promoted only if it is significantly better on the same questions. Comparing two accuracies separately would hide that only the questions where the models disagree carry information. Both models ran on the same RTX 4090; 9B used on-the-fly FP8 weights.
